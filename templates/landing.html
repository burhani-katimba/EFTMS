What is CouncilDMS?

Every file tracked. Every step clear. Every citizen informed.

CouncilDMS eliminates paper-based bottlenecks by digitizing the entire document lifecycle — from the moment a citizen submits a file to the moment it is collected.

  • Citizens submit documents online or at the registry — both routes lead into the same digital pipeline.
  • Registrars review, register, and forward to the correct department with a single click.
  • Departments process, sign, and forward to the Director for final approval — all within the system.
  • Applicants receive email notifications at every stage and can track progress in real time.

Two Submission Paths

Online upload for digital users, or physical scan at the registry counter.

QR Code per File

Every registered document gets a unique QR code printed and attached to the physical file.

Digital Signatures

Officers and the Director sign documents electronically. Signatures become part of the final record.

Municipal Stamp

The Director applies the official municipal stamp at final approval for full authenticity.

Document Lifecycle

Ten stages from start to finish.

1

Submitted

Citizen uploads online or registry scans physical document.

2

Under Review

Registrar reviews document, edits details, confirms registration.

3

Registered

Document ID and QR code generated. Ready for forwarding.

4

Forwarded to Dept

Sent to the appropriate department for processing.

5

Dept Review

Department officer reviews, comments, and adds supporting files.

6

Awaiting Director

Department signs and forwards to the Director's desk.

7

Approved

Director approves with signature and municipal stamp.

8

Returned to Registry

Approved document comes back for finalization.

9

Ready for Collection

Printed, stamped, waiting for the applicant.

10

Collected

Citizen collects the finalized document. Cycle complete.

Who Uses This System

Five roles, one unified workflow.

Citizen

Submits documents, tracks progress, receives notifications, and collects finalized files.

Registrar

Registers physical and digital submissions, reviews PDFs, forwards to departments.

Dept Officer

Reviews assigned files, comments, signs, and forwards processed documents to the Director.

Director

Final approval authority. Signs, stamps, and returns documents to the registry.

Administrator

Manages users, departments, roles, and views system-wide reports and activity logs.

Quick Guide

How to submit, track, and collect your documents.

1

Submit a Document

Log in to your account, fill in the document details, upload the file, and select the department. Your document is immediately registered in the system.

Alternatively, visit the Registry Office with your physical document — they will scan and register it for you.

2

Track Progress

Use your Document ID or scan the QR code on your physical file to see the current status. Every stage change triggers an email notification.

The timeline shows every action taken on your document along with the officer responsible.

3

Collect Your File

When the status changes to "Ready for Collection", visit the Registry Office. Present your Document ID or the QR code for quick retrieval.

For softcopy submissions, collection may not be required — the approved digital copy is available for download.
